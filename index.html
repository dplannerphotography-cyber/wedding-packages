Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

When does this promotional price apply?

This special pricing is valid for all wedding dates booked and secured between September and December 2026. Dates are reserved strictly on a first-come, first-served basis upon receipt of a booking deposit.

Do you travel outside our state/city for weddings?

Yes, DPlanner Photography is available for travel nationwide across Nigeria and destination weddings internationally! Travel and accommodation arrangements are quoted simply and transparently.

How soon will our pictures and videos be ready?

You will receive a curated selection of "Sneak Peek" photos within 48 to 72 hours of your wedding! Your complete high-resolution gallery and edited cinematic films are delivered within 2 to 4 weeks.

Can we customize or add extra services to a package?

Absolutely! You can add extra drone aerial coverage, live streaming, additional parent photo albums, or photo booth setups. Just message us directly on WhatsApp and we will create a tailored proposal for you.
