### Part 2: How They Met & The Proposal Narrative
- A short, authentic two-paragraph story recounting:

### Part 3: Cultural Styling, Fabrics & Attire
- **Traditional Attire Details**:
  * Color palette chosen (e.g., regal wine and champagne, burnt orange and teal, royal emerald green and gold).

  * Gele styling, beads, and groom's Agbada embroidery motif.
- **White Wedding Attire Details**:
  * Bridal gown silhouette, veil length, bouquet flowers.
  * Groom's bespoke suit or tuxedo tailoring.

### Part 4: Traditional Rites & The Emotional Highlights
- Highlights of the cultural engagement rites:

  * The proposal letter reading and exchange of rings.
- Key quote from the father or mother of the bride during prayers.

### Part 7: Delivered Heirlooms & Turnaround Timeline
- Exact deliverables confirmed for the couple:

  * Complete color-graded digital gallery delivered within **3–4 weeks**.
  * Physical heirlooms: 12×24 luxury flush-mount lay-flat album housed in a customized matching briefcase.
  * Large framed portraits installed in the couple's home.
