## Article Outline Overview

### Section 1: Introduction to Pre-Wedding Photography in Ibadan
- The purpose of a pre-wedding shoot: building rapport with your photographer, testing your hair and makeup in natural light, and creating images for save-the-date invitations, wedding websites, and reception welcome banners.
- Why Ibadan offers unmatched diversity: combining colonial architectural heritage, lush tropical canopies, quiet lakes, and contemporary studio environments within short driving distances.

### Section 2: Top 6 Recommended Locations in Ibadan

#### Location 1: Agodi Gardens (Parliament Road, Mokola Hill)
- **Aesthetic**: Lush forestry, paved botanical walkways, wooden bridges, manicured lawns, and soft filtered canopy light.
- **Best Time to Shoot**: 7:30 AM – 9:30 AM (quiet mornings before public crowds arrive) or 4:30 PM – 6:00 PM (golden hour).
- **Ideal Outfits**: Casual chic (linen shirts, sundresses, coordinated neutral tones) or flowing editorial evening gowns.
- **Logistics & Permits**: Modest entrance and commercial photography permit fee payable at the main gate.

#### Location 2: International Institute of Tropical Agriculture (IITA, Idi-Ose)
- **Aesthetic**: Sprawling 1,000-hectare research sanctuary featuring serene lakes, tree-lined avenues, golf course greenery, and secluded forested lanes.
- **Best Time to Shoot**: Mid-afternoon leading into sunset over the lake.
- **Ideal Outfits**: Elegant formal wear, tailored suits, and sophisticated cultural casuals.
- **Logistics & Permits**: Prior reservation or weekend guest pass required at the security gate; commercial photography requires advance authorization.

#### Location 3: University of Ibadan Botanical & Zoological Gardens (UI, Agbowo)
- **Aesthetic**: Towering mahogany and teak trees, shaded botanical paths, historic university architecture, and rustic natural benches.
- **Best Time to Shoot**: Early morning hours (8:00 AM – 10:00 AM) to catch sunlight cutting through mist.
- **Ideal Outfits**: Scholarly or relaxed casuals; vibrant colors that pop against deep forest greens.
- **Logistics & Permits**: Standard campus entry protocol with nominal photography clearance fee.

#### Location 4: Mapo Hall & Historic Old Ibadan (Oja'ba)
- **Aesthetic**: Imposing neoclassical colonial pillars, grand stone staircases, and historic architectural grandeur perched high above the ancient rusted-roof cityscape.
- **Best Time to Shoot**: Early Sunday mornings (when central market traffic is light).
- **Ideal Outfits**: Royal traditional attire (rich Aso-Oke, Agbada, and modern vintage tailoring).
- **Logistics & Permits**: Coordination with local council gate stewards.

#### Location 5: Bower’s Tower (Oke-Are Hill)
- **Aesthetic**: Panoramic 360-degree hilltop views of Ibadan’s skyline, stone tower monument, and dramatic open sky.
- **Best Time to Shoot**: Sunset golden hour (5:00 PM – 6:15 PM) for dramatic silhouettes against the horizon.
- **Ideal Outfits**: Dramatic flowing dresses with fabric that catches the hilltop breeze.

#### Location 6: DPlanner Photography Studio (82 De ACE HALL, Yemetu)
- **Aesthetic**: Controlled indoor studio environment with professional strobe lighting, diverse solid-color backdrops, comfortable air conditioning, and complete privacy for outfit changes.
- **Best Time to Shoot**: Flexible scheduling (Monday to Saturday from 9:00 AM, select Sundays from 3:00 PM).
- **Ideal Outfits**: High-fashion studio looks, clean editorial portraits, and themed creative concepts.
- **Delivery Guarantee**: Standard studio turnaround within 24 hours of selection (same-day express available for +₦500/pic).

### Section 3: Essential Logistics Checklist for Couples
1. **Weather Planning**: Navigating the dry harmattan season (November–February) vs. lush rainy season (April–October).
2. **Footwear Tip**: Bring comfortable flats or slip-ons for walking between outdoor spots, changing into heels only for the actual shots.
3. **Touch-Up Kit**: Portable powder, blotting paper, and setting spray to combat Ibadan humidity.
4. **Hydration & Refreshments**: Keep a small cooler with cold water and snacks in your car.

### Section 4: Combining On-Location & Studio Looks
- How many couples do a split shoot: 1 hour of controlled studio portraits at our Yemetu studio, followed by an outdoor golden hour session at Agodi Gardens or UI.
