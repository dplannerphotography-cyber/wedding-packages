Your wedding photographs and highlight film are the only tangible legacy of your wedding day that outlives the ceremony. You cannot restage your groom's tears when he first sees you walking down the aisle, nor can you reenact your parents' heartfelt blessings during the engagement prostration (*Dobale*).

In Ibadan, where hundreds of photographers advertise on Instagram and TikTok, choosing the right person to document your celebration can feel overwhelming. A visually appealing social media feed is not always proof of professional reliability on a chaotic wedding day.

To help you protect your investment and secure peace of mind, here are **10 essential questions you must ask any wedding photographer in Ibadan before paying a deposit**.

---

## 1. "Can we see a full wedding gallery, not just your best 10 Instagram highlights?"

Anyone with a modern mirrorless camera can capture 10 beautiful photos over the course of an eight-hour wedding. 

What separates a seasoned wedding photographer from an amateur is **consistency under difficult conditions**:
- Dimly lit hotel rooms during bridal prep
- Harried lighting during church processions
- Flash management in cavernous banquet halls (such as Jogor Centre or Manhattan Hall)
- Late-night outdoor receptions

**What to look for**: Ask to see a link to an entire client gallery of 300 to 500 pictures. Look at how the photographer handles skin tones, motion blur during dancing, and candid moments when subjects aren't posing.

---

## 2. "What are your exact, written delivery turnaround times?"

This is the single most important question you will ask. It is surprisingly common in Nigeria for couples to wait four, six, or even twelve months to receive their pictures or wedding video.

**Ask for firm dates in your contract**:
- When will a preview or highlight film be ready?
- When will the full edited gallery be delivered?
- What happens if the photographer misses the deadline?

## 3. "How do you handle natural skin retouching and diverse complexions?"

Traditional Nigerian weddings are vibrant celebrations of rich complexions and intricate ceremonial attire: wine, teal, royal blue, and gold Aso-Oke, coupled with heavy beadwork and tailored velvet.

Inexperienced photographers frequently rely on global "light and airy" presets that wash out melanin, turn deep skin tones greyish, or blur skin textures until subjects look plastic.

**What to ask**: Inquire about their post-processing philosophy. At DPlanner, we practice **natural skin retouching**. We remove temporary blemishes while preserving true skin texture, realistic complexions, and the authentic weave of your cultural fabrics.

## 6. "Are you experienced with Ibadan and Oyo State event venues?"

Lighting conditions vary dramatically across Ibadan's premier wedding venues:
- **Jogor Centre (Ring Road)**: Massive hall requiring powerful remote speedlights and bounce flashes.
- **The Civic Centre (Idi-Ape)**: High ceilings that demand strategic optical setups.
- **Manhattan Hall (Jericho)**: Sophisticated interior with dim ambient lighting that requires fast prime lenses.
- **Felicia Hall / Jogor**: Specific acoustic and stage constraints.

A photographer who regularly shoots in Ibadan knows the traffic choke points around Mokola, Challenge, and Iwo Road, knows when to arrive for setup, and knows how to light each specific ballroom.

## 7. "Do you offer cinematic highlight films in addition to photography?"

Modern wedding video is no longer a monotonous three-hour unedited recording. Today's couples want a **cinematic 3- to 5-minute highlight film** set to music, featuring crisp audio recordings of your vows and speeches, cut dynamically for sharing on Instagram, WhatsApp, and family group chats.

Always confirm whether the videographer captures dedicated cinema footage or merely recorded raw clips, and check if microphone audio for vows is included.

## 8. "What type of photobook album is included, and how is it protected?"

Not all photo albums are created equal. Many studios supply press-printed books that warp or stick together when exposed to Nigerian humidity.

**Ask specific album questions**:
- Is it a genuine flush-mount album with thick lay-flat photographic pages?
- What are the dimensions? (Our standard is a generous **12×24 inches**).
- Does it include a protective storage case?

At DPlanner, our **Signature Album Package (₦450,000)** and our standalone **Album Collection Add-on (₦180,000)** include a handcrafted 12×24 flush-mount album housed in a **customized luxury briefcase** designed to protect your prints against moisture and dust.

## 9. "What are your payment terms and deposit requirements?"

Be wary of photographers who demand 100% upfront payment before the wedding date. Professional studios operate with clear, balanced contractual milestones.

**Standard industry practice**:
- A **50% deposit** secures the date on the calendar (non-refundable, as the studio turns away other inquiries for your date).
- The remaining **50% balance** is due before final delivery of the edited gallery and films.

## 10. "What backup equipment do you bring on the day?"

Cameras are electronic devices; memory cards can corrupt, batteries can drain, and lenses can suffer accidental drops.

Ask your photographer directly:
- *"Do your cameras record to dual memory card slots simultaneously?"* (At DPlanner, every photo is written to two separate memory cards at the exact moment the shutter clicks).
- *"Do you carry backup camera bodies, flashes, and lenses on site?"*

A true professional will always have redundant equipment on site so that a technical glitch never stops your wedding documentation.
